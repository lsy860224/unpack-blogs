## TL;DR

AIGrit publishes Korean-language AI tool guides that compare tools by official documentation, pricing, policy, and real use cases. We write — we don't sell.

## Editorial principles

1. **Sources** — features, pricing, and policies are checked against official documentation and linked. Time-sensitive facts carry the date they were checked.
2. **Honesty** — no speed or accuracy scores we didn't measure in a controlled test, and no income claims we can't verify. Personal experience is labeled as experience.
3. **Comparison** — 1:1 or N:N comparisons preferred over solo write-ups. Alternatives are always included.
4. **Context** — we specify "as an office worker" / "as a developer" scenarios so readers can map their own situation.

These principles were revised in October 2026. Earlier versions promised hands-on testing with sample sizes that many posts did not actually meet, so those posts were rewritten from official sources and this page was corrected. See the [Korean original](/ko/blog/hello-world) for details.
